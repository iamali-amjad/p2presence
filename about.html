Our story

Beautiful stores that actually sell.

P2 Presence started with a simple frustration: too many stores looked great but didn't convert. We set out to close that gap — pairing conversion-first design with clean engineering and honest, data-led decisions.

Seven years and forty-plus stores later, the belief is the same: every visit should work harder for your revenue. No jargon, no bloat, no disappearing after launch.
