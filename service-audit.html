Overview

Shopify Audit

Before you spend on a redesign, know exactly what's worth fixing. Our audit reviews your store like a customer, a developer, and a marketer would — then hands you a ranked plan.

Every recommendation is scored by impact and effort, so you invest in the work that moves your numbers first.

What's included

  • UX & Conversion ReviewAn evaluation of the experience and conversion paths that shape trust and engagement.
  • Performance & Speed AnalysisTechnical analysis of the front-end and structural bottlenecks slowing you down.
  • Theme & Code ReviewA review of theme structure, code quality, and long-term maintainability.
  • App Stack EvaluationAn assessment of installed apps to find conflicts, redundancy, and savings.
  • Actionable RecommendationsA clear, prioritized plan scored by impact, effort, and expected outcome.
