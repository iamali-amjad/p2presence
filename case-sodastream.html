Project overview

The brief.

SodaStream is a food & drink brand with real momentum. They came to us to turn a good product into a store that sells as well as it looks — faster, clearer, and built to grow.

The challenge

What was holding it back.

High traffic from paid channels that wasn't converting, and no system to turn first orders into repeat revenue.

Our solution

What we did.

  • Rebuilt the storefront on a fast, conversion-first Shopify 2.0 theme.
  • Streamlined the path to checkout and sharpened product-page storytelling.
  • Optimized Core Web Vitals so the store stays fast on mobile.
  • Set up tracking and a testing roadmap to keep improving after launch.
